Privacy

My Future Self · last updated 27 November 2026

What is saved

  • From your Google account: your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. Nothing else, and never your password.
  • What you enter in the app: your habits and ticks, salah ticks, daily actions (feel, productivity, energy, money made and spent, time distracted), your money total and your settings.

Where it is saved

In the app's database on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and D1), linked to your Google account. A copy is also kept in your own browser so the app opens fast and keeps working when you are offline. Only you, signed in with your Google account, can see your data.

Why

Only to run the app for you: to sign you in and to show you your own data on every device. Your data is not sold, shared or used for adverts.

Cookies

One cookie, mfs_session, keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is needed for the app to work. There are no tracking or advert cookies.

Other services the app uses

  • Google sign-in (accounts.google.com), when you sign in.
  • Google Fonts, for the app's fonts.
  • api.alquran.cloud, for the daily Quran verse.
  • api.quotable.kurokeita.dev, for motivation quotes.
  • namazvakitleri.com.tr, for Coventry prayer times (fetched by the app's server, not by your browser).

None of them receive your entries.

Deleting your data

Open the app, click your account picture next to the Settings gear, and choose Delete my account. Your account and everything you saved are deleted from the database straight away and cannot be brought back. Signing out also removes the copy in that browser.
